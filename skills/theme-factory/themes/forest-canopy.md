# Forest Canopy Theme

A natural and grounded theme inspired by woodland environments.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex Code |
|------|-------|----------|
| Primary | Forest Green | `#276749` |
| Secondary | Moss | `#68d391` |
| Accent | Bark Brown | `#744210` |
| Background | Soft Sage | `#f0fff4` |
| Text | Deep Forest | `#1c4532` |
| Muted Text | Stone | `#718096` |

## Font Pairing

- **Headers**: Merriweather (serif) - traditional and trustworthy
- **Body**: Lato (sans-serif) - warm and stable

## Usage

Best suited for:
- Environmental presentations
- Wellness and health content
- Sustainability reports
- Outdoor/nature brands

The earthy tones create a sense of growth, stability, and natural authenticity.
