# Arctic Frost Theme

A cool and crisp winter-inspired theme with icy elegance.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex Code |
|------|-------|----------|
| Primary | Ice Blue | `#4299e1` |
| Secondary | Glacier | `#90cdf4` |
| Accent | Arctic White | `#e2e8f0` |
| Background | Snow | `#f7fafc` |
| Text | Midnight Blue | `#2a4365` |
| Muted Text | Frost Gray | `#a0aec0` |

## Font Pairing

- **Headers**: Raleway (sans-serif) - light and airy
- **Body**: Roboto (sans-serif) - clean and modern

## Usage

Best suited for:
- Winter events
- Technology companies
- Healthcare presentations
- Scientific reports

The cool palette creates a sense of clarity, precision, and professionalism.
