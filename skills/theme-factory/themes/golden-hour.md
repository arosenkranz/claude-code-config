# Golden Hour Theme

A rich and warm autumnal palette inspired by late afternoon light.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex Code |
|------|-------|----------|
| Primary | Amber | `#b7791f` |
| Secondary | Burnt Sienna | `#c05621` |
| Accent | Gold | `#ecc94b` |
| Background | Warm Ivory | `#fffff0` |
| Text | Deep Brown | `#553c08` |
| Muted Text | Taupe | `#9c8860` |

## Font Pairing

- **Headers**: Cormorant Garamond (serif) - sophisticated and elegant
- **Body**: Nunito (sans-serif) - rounded and friendly

## Usage

Best suited for:
- Luxury brands
- Autumn/fall events
- Food and beverage
- Artisanal products

The golden tones convey warmth, quality, and timeless elegance.
