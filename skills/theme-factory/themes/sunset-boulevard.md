# Sunset Boulevard Theme

A warm and vibrant theme inspired by golden hour sunsets.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex Code |
|------|-------|----------|
| Primary | Sunset Orange | `#dd6b20` |
| Secondary | Golden Yellow | `#d69e2e` |
| Accent | Coral Pink | `#fc8181` |
| Background | Warm Cream | `#fffaf0` |
| Text | Deep Brown | `#2d3748` |
| Muted Text | Dusty Rose | `#a0aec0` |

## Font Pairing

- **Headers**: Montserrat (sans-serif) - bold and modern
- **Body**: Open Sans (sans-serif) - friendly and approachable

## Usage

Best suited for:
- Creative presentations
- Marketing materials
- Event invitations
- Lifestyle brands

The warm palette creates energy and optimism while remaining professional.
