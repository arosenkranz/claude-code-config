## Entry Format Templates

### Project Entry

```markdown
### [YYYY-MM] - [Project Name]
[1-2 sentence description of what you did]

**Impact**: [Quantifiable results or qualitative outcomes]
**Technologies**: [Key tools/technologies used]
**Collaboration**: [Teams/people you worked with]
```

### Collaboration Entry

```markdown
### [YYYY-MM] - [Activity/Initiative]
[Description of mentorship, collaboration, or leadership]

**Outcome**: [Result of the collaboration]
```

### Learning Entry

```markdown
### [YYYY-MM] - [Skill/Technology]
[What you learned and context]

**Applied to**: [How you used this learning]
```

## Impact Statement Patterns

### Quantifiable Impact

```markdown
❌ VAGUE: "Improved system performance"
✅ SPECIFIC: "Reduced API response time from 800ms to 200ms (75% improvement)"

❌ VAGUE: "Helped team be more productive"
✅ SPECIFIC: "Automated deployment process, reducing release time from 2 hours to 15 minutes"

❌ VAGUE: "Made documentation better"
✅ SPECIFIC: "Rewrote onboarding docs, decreasing new hire setup time from 3 days to 4 hours"
```

### Qualitative Impact (for managers)

```markdown
✅ "Improved team psychological safety through weekly retrospectives,
   with team engagement scores increasing from 3.2 to 4.5 out of 5"

✅ "Established cross-functional working group that resolved 6-month
   platform dependency blocker"

✅ "Mentored junior engineer through first major project, resulting in
   successful promotion to mid-level"
```

## Content Guidelines

### What to Include

**Projects:**
* Significant features or initiatives you led/contributed to
* Problems you solved
* Systems you built or improved
* Process improvements

**Collaboration:**
* Mentoring and coaching
* Cross-team initiatives
* Unblocking others
* Knowledge sharing

**Growth:**
* New skills acquired
* Technologies learned
* Courses completed
* Certifications earned

**Outside Work:**
* Conference talks
* Blog posts
* Open source contributions
* Community involvement

### What to Avoid

* Daily routine tasks
* Tasks any team member would do
* Vague accomplishments without context
* Exaggerations or unverifiable claims

## Prompting for Details

When expanding an entry, ask:

**For Projects:**
* What was the measurable impact?
* How did this help the team/company/customers?
* What was the scope? (users affected, time saved, etc.)

**For Managerial Work:**
* How did the team improve?
* What process enhancements resulted?
* How did this contribute to culture/morale?
* What strategic decisions were made?

## Update Frequency

* **After major milestones**: Document while details are fresh
* **Monthly reviews**: Scan recent work for brag-worthy items
* **Quarterly summaries**: Review and categorize accumulated entries
* **Performance review prep**: Comprehensive review and organization

## Tips for Consistency

1. **Use consistent date format**: YYYY-MM or Month Year
2. **Keep entries brief**: 1-2 sentences per entry
3. **Focus on impact**: Always include outcomes
4. **Be specific**: Use numbers when possible
5. **Update "Last Updated"** date when modifying

## Review Questions

Before adding an entry, ask:

* Would this impress my manager?
* Can I quantify the impact?
* Does this show growth or initiative?
* Is this unique to my contributions?
* Will I remember why this mattered in 6 months?
